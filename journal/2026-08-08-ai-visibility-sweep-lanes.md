# Lane 1 — Yelp → ChatGPT (landed 2026-08-08)

FINDINGS

- Yelp-OpenAI licensing deal confirmed: operational partnership announced July 23, 2026 via Axios exclusive ("Yelp deal pushes local reviews into ChatGPT"), but the underlying licensing agreement was signed earlier and first disclosed in Yelp's FY2025 results published February 12, 2026 — so "July 2026" is when the ChatGPT integration was announced, not when the deal was signed → https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/yelp-reviews-chatgpt-geo-partnership (2026-07-23) [INDEPENDENT]
- Deal scope: Yelp licenses reviews (330M cumulative user-generated reviews), ratings, photos, and business listing details (8M+ business listings), described in coverage as giving ChatGPT "real-time local recommendation data"; financial terms undisclosed; contract is explicitly non-exclusive (Yelp free to strike similar deals with other AI platforms) → https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/yelp-stock-pops-8-openai-134925071.html and https://searchengineland.com/openai-yelp-deal-483326 (2026-07-23) [INDEPENDENT]
- Live status per Yelp itself: Q2 2026 shareholder letter (8-K exhibit, earnings reported Aug 6, 2026) says Yelp "ratings and reviews recently began powering ChatGPT's local experience in relevant categories" — i.e., live as of early August 2026 but only in unspecified "relevant categories"; CEO Stoppelman called the OpenAI relationship "still early" but potentially "a meaningful distribution channel over time" → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001345016/000134501626000059/yelpq22026ex992lettertos.htm and https://m.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-yelp-beats-q2-2026-revenue-forecast-as-shares-rise-after-hours-93CH-4844784 (2026-08-06) [VENDOR]
- Revenue context: Yelp's "Other revenue" (Hatch + data licensing + food ordering) grew 98% YoY to $33M in Q2 2026, with a stated $250M annual run-rate goal by 2028 — data licensing to AI platforms is now a strategic Yelp revenue line, meaning Yelp is incentivized to push data into more AI surfaces → https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9015188/yelp-inc-yelp-q2-2026-earnings-call-highlights-strong-revenue-and-ebitda-beat-ai-initiatives-drive-engagement (2026-08-06) [INDEPENDENT]
- Presentation mechanics: ChatGPT local answers can include Yelp reviews, ratings, photos, and business details with Yelp branding and links/attribution shown when content is used — but multiple outlets note OpenAI (not Yelp) controls how and when that experience is presented; no source describes a dedicated Yelp results panel, and no source confirms whether the web-search toggle must be active for Yelp data to appear → https://martech.org/yelp-brings-reviews-and-local-leads-to-chatgpt/ (2026-07-23) [INDEPENDENT]
- Lead-gen extension: Yelp's "Request a Quote" feature is planned ("coming soon" as of the announcement) inside ChatGPT local-services searches, letting users contact participating service merchants directly from AI answers — the first transactional (not just citation) Yelp surface in ChatGPT → https://www.citybiz.co/article/878630/yelp-licenses-reviews-and-business-data-to-openai-for-chatgpt-integration/ (2026-07) [INDEPENDENT]
- ChatGPT local UI context: OpenAI's help docs state ChatGPT search "may output a map if the result is appropriate" on iOS/Android, and location sharing improves "near me" results; vendor analysis claims ChatGPT's local/map results are Mapbox-powered rather than Google Maps → https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search (ongoing, 2025-2026) [VENDOR] and https://graybox.co/knowledge/blog/getting-your-business-on-the-map-in-chatgpt-why-mapbox-listings-matter (2025-2026) [VENDOR]
- Pipeline mechanism: Yelp sells a productized "AI Agents" data-licensing solution (Yelp AI API + Model Context Protocol server) for feeding real-time Yelp data into AI assistants — the same commercial pipeline family used by Perplexity (Yelp Places API); Yelp's blog says its licensing contracts govern permitted uses and require partners to promptly purge deleted content, implying removed reviews should drop out of ChatGPT → https://business.yelp.com/data/solutions/ai-agents/ and https://blog.yelp.com/news/how-yelp-continues-to-protect-your-content-in-the-age-of-ai/ (2025-2026) [VENDOR]
- Yelp-side factors, per Yelp's own trust rules: Yelp forbids review solicitation and filters suspected prompted reviews; in 2025 its systems filtered ~500K reviews exhibiting AI-generated characteristics — so "get more reviews fast" tactics are policy-violating and filtered content never reaches the licensed feed; compliant levers are accurate/complete profile info, responding to reviews, and organic review accumulation → https://trust.yelp.com/trust-and-safety-report/2025-report/ and https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225039344/en/Yelp-Releases-2025-Trust-Safety-Report (2026-02-25) [VENDOR]
- Vendor consensus on actionable factors (no verified thresholds anywhere): keep profile claimed/complete with accurate hours, maintain NAP consistency across directories, fresh reviews and photos, respond to all reviews (responses "feed AI models more recent, detailed, authentic text"), and mixed-but-answered reviews may read as more genuine to models than uniform 5-stars → https://synpost.synup.com/yelp-is-now-inside-chatgpt-most-of-you-can-relax/ (2026-07/08), https://seovendor.co/chatgpt-gets-yelp-data-local-geo-agencies-must-adapt/ (2026), https://www.mickmel.com/yelp-is-becoming-huge-for-chatgpt/ (2026-07) [VENDOR]
- Citation-share data point: 5W Research's Q1 2026 Citation Source Audit (synthesizing Similarweb, SEMrush, Profound, Peec AI, SE Ranking, Ahrefs et al. datasets) found Wikipedia+Reddit drive >25% of US ChatGPT citations, and brands with profiles on review platforms including Yelp saw a ~3x citation multiplier vs. brands without — pre-dating the licensed integration, so Yelp's share is likely higher now → https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wikipedia-and-reddit-now-drive-over-25-of-chatgpt-citations-in-the-us-new-5w-research-finds--wsj-nyt-and-bloomberg-do-not-appear-in-the-top-20-302768339.html (Q1-Q2 2026) [VENDOR]
- Independent pre-deal testing (why the deal matters): NYC restaurant critic Ryan Sutton's hands-on test ("Don't Ask ChatGPT for Restaurant Advice!", The LO Times) found ChatGPT invented dishes and fictional restaurants next to real ones, stated claims as fact without citations, and fabricated taste descriptions — the hallucination problem Yelp's licensed data is explicitly pitched as fixing → https://www.thelotimes.com/p/chat-gpt-restaurant-advice-reviews-open-ai (2024) [INDEPENDENT]
- Market signal: Yelp shares rose ~8% on the July 23 announcement day; framing across coverage: Yelp treats ChatGPT "the way it previously treated Apple Maps or Alexa — as just another distribution platform," contrasting with NYT's litigation approach → https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/yelp-stock-pops-8-openai-134925071.html (2026-07-23) [INDEPENDENT]
- What Yelp tells business owners: no dedicated official "AI visibility playbook" from Yelp was found; Yelp's public messaging to owners remains claim-your-free-page basics (biz.yelp.com/claim: respond to reviews, reply to quote requests, update business info) plus its content-protection/licensing blog posts — the AI-visibility advice ecosystem is almost entirely third-party SEO vendors, not Yelp → https://business.yelp.com/products/business-page/ and https://www.yelp-support.com/article/What-is-a-claimed-business?l=en_US (ongoing) [VENDOR]

UNKNOWNS
- Whether Yelp data surfaces only when ChatGPT's web-search/grounding path triggers, vs. being injected via a dedicated server-side feed regardless of the search toggle — no source specifies the technical trigger condition.
- Whether claimed vs. unclaimed status affects inclusion in the licensed feed (the 8M+ listing feed appears to cover all listings; no source says claiming changes eligibility — claiming plausibly affects only data accuracy/completeness, unverified).
- Whether Yelp ad spend or Request-a-Quote participation status influences which businesses ChatGPT surfaces — no source addresses monetization/ranking mechanics; "participating merchants" for Request a Quote is undefined.
- Which "relevant categories" are live, rollout geography (presumably US-heavy given Yelp's footprint), and exact go-live date of the in-product experience.
- Financial terms of the license, and whether OpenAI may train models on Yelp data vs. display-time grounding only.
- No rigorous independent post-deal measurement of Yelp appearance/citation rates in ChatGPT local answers exists yet (integration ~2 weeks old at research time); nearest data is pre-deal citation studies.
- Verification caveat: the sandbox egress proxy blocked WebFetch/curl for effectively all news and Yelp domains, so every claim above is sourced from search-engine result summaries of those URLs, not full article text.

# Lane 2 — GBP → Gemini / Google AI (landed 2026-08-08)

FINDINGS

- Gemini now pulls local-business results directly from Google Maps and renders them as visual place cards (photos, ratings, hours, location) inside Gemini answers — announced by Google Dec 13, 2025 → https://ppc.land/gemini-now-serves-local-results-from-google-maps-in-visual-format/ → 2025-12-13 → [INDEPENDENT] (trade press reporting a Google announcement)
- Google officially connected GBP to the Gemini app (June 10, 2026, The Keyword, Vishnu Sivaji): owners one-tap-link their profile, then use natural language to update hours, post updates, draft review replies, and analyze impressions/calls/direction requests; edits made via Gemini go live on the profile; initial rollout = Gemini web app, single-profile accounts, plus "Business notebooks" → https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-features-for-businesses/ and https://9to5google.com/2026/06/10/gemini-google-business-profile/ → 2026-06-10 → [VENDOR] (Google first-party; 9to5Google corroboration is [INDEPENDENT])
- Multi-location GBP accounts gained Gemini management support after the single-profile launch; Google also documents Gemini-web-app profile management in official GBP Help (support.google.com/business/answer/17142585) → https://ppc.land/google-business-profile-in-gemini-now-works-for-multi-location-accounts/ → 2026 (post-June) → [INDEPENDENT] / Google help doc [VENDOR-first-party]
- Google's only official optimization guidance ("Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search," announced by John Mueller, May 2026): AI Overviews/AI Mode are "rooted in core Search ranking and quality systems," no special markup, llms.txt, or AI-specific files are used or rewarded, "AEO/GEO is still SEO"; the guide includes tips for local content but publishes no GBP-field-level ranking detail → https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/05/a-new-resource-for-optimizing and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide → 2026-05 → [VENDOR] (Google first-party)
- Whitespark prevalence study (540 manual queries, Houston/Phoenix/Denver, 6 service industries incl. plumbers): AI Overviews appear on 68% of local-business queries vs local pack on 39%; but explicit "who do I call"-style local intent ("service + city," "near me") flips it — local pack 93% / AIO only 15%; informational-local 92% AIO, hybrid ("average cost of dental implants in Phoenix") 97% AIO → https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/ (covered by SEJ https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-now-answer-most-local-searches-how-to-get-your-business-cited/580757/) → study 2026; SEJ ~2026-07 → [VENDOR] (sells local SEO tools/services)
- Local Falcon whitepaper (60,000 scan simulations, 4,423 businesses, 20 countries): AI Overviews do NOT rank local businesses by distance (no distance-position correlation), but proximity still gates inclusion (72.0% appearance closest vs 68.5% farther, within a 4-mile radius); AIO local results are volatile across searcher location, time of day, and phrasing → https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/whitepaper-studies-the-impact-of-google-ai-overviews-on-local-business-search-visibility / press release https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/21/3085651/0/en/ → 2025-05-21 → [VENDOR] (sells geo-grid rank tracking)
- Local SEO Data study (Noah Owsiany; 1,120 query executions, May 20–22 2026, 7 verticals × 13 US cities): 28.5% of Local Pack businesses never appear in AI Mode (71.5% overlap, 95% CI 69.2–73.8%); top-3 vs top-3 overlap only 48%; 999 businesses surfaced in AI Mode that never appeared in the Local Pack; appending "near me" restored business listings in AI Mode in 100% of tested cases (38/38) — i.e., local-pack rank helps but does not carry over 1:1 → https://www.prweb.com/releases/local-seo-data-study-finds-28-5-of-local-pack-businesses-are-invisible-in-google-ai-mode-302796340.html → 2026-06 → [VENDOR] (local SEO tool company)
- Whitespark 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors (expert survey, first edition with a formal "AI Search Visibility" category): Local Pack weights = GBP signals 32%, reviews 20% (up from 16% in 2023), on-page 15%; AI Search Visibility weights differ = on-page 24%, reviews 16%, citations 13%, links 13%, GBP signals only 12%; 3 of top-5 AI-visibility factors are citation-type (best-of lists, industry-domain prominence, unstructured mentions in news/gov/associations) → https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/ → 2026 edition → [VENDOR]
- AI Mode leans heavily on review data (volume + recency + text content) and NAP consistency for local answers; AI Mode uses "query fan-out" (multiple parallel sub-queries, reported up to ~16) against core ranking systems, then synthesizes — Whitespark's AI Mode guide; note the review examples are practitioner heuristics, not measured coefficients → https://whitespark.ca/guides/whitesparks-guide-to-googles-ai-mode-for-local-businesses/ → 2025-2026 → [VENDOR]
- Citation analysis of 28M+ AI responses to local queries (Q4 2025, across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode): Yelp took 512,680 citations — 3.4x #2 BBB (149,710), then Angi (145,633), Thumbtack (56,004); within Google AI Mode, Yelp captured 72.5% of citations among the local-directory competitive set — Google's own AI heavily cites third-party review platforms alongside/instead of GBP → https://ppc.land/yelp-gets-3-4x-more-ai-citations-than-any-rival-in-new-local-search-data/ and https://foundationinc.co/lab/yelp-ai-local-discovery/ → data Q4 2025, reported early 2026 → [VENDOR] risk (underlying dataset likely Yelp-affiliated; see UNKNOWNS)
- Gemini generates its own interpretive layer on top of GBP data — tested early 2026, Gemini's local results show AI-written business headlines and sections like "People talk most about" synthesized from review content, meaning review text (not just star ratings) directly shapes how the business is described → https://www.seroundtable.com/google-gemini-local-results-40741.html → early 2026 → [INDEPENDENT] (trade press)
- Gemini is not GBP-exclusive: when Local Falcon probed Gemini on where it gets local business info, it self-reported GBP, business websites, and directories, and in live tests sourced a location's hours from TripAdvisor rather than GBP — GBP is necessary but not sufficient → https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/where-does-gemini-get-local-business-info → 2024-2025 → [VENDOR]
- AI Overview "local packs" (place-card units embedded in AIOs, expanding late 2025 into 2026) surface only ~32% as many unique businesses as the legacy 3-pack — typically 1-2 businesses, no call buttons — concentrating visibility in fewer winners; some businesses report 50%+ visibility drops → https://onpurposemedia.com/ai-overview-local-packs-impacting-visibility/ → late 2025/2026 → [VENDOR] (agency)
- Semrush tracking (10M+ keywords, 2025): AI Overview presence rose from ~6.5% of tracked searches (Jan 2025) to a ~24.6% peak (Jul) settling ~15.7% (Nov 2025) — AIO exposure for local categories grew through 2025 but remains query-type dependent → https://searchlab.nl/en/statistics/ai-overviews-sge-statistics-2026 (aggregating Semrush data) → 2025 data / 2026 publication → [VENDOR]
- Birdeye "State of Google Business Profile 2026" (enterprise benchmark report): 76% of profiles now verified; AI-powered search is reducing raw GBP impressions while high-value actions (calls, directions) hold — consistent with fewer-but-higher-intent AI-era surfaces → https://birdeye.com/blog/state-of-google-business-profiles/ → 2026 → [VENDOR] (sells review/listing management)

UNKNOWNS

- Propagation latency: no credible source quantifies how fast a GBP edit (hours, category, services, new reviews) propagates into Gemini answers, AI Overviews, or AI Mode; Google publishes no SLA and no vendor has measured it. Gemini's Dec-2025 Maps integration implies near-live Maps reads for place cards, but the AI-synthesis layer's refresh cadence is unverified.
- The widely repeated claim that GBP became "the primary data layer feeding Gemini, Search, and Maps per Google's internal docs" (April 2026) appears only in vendor blogs (mapranks.com, digitalapplied.com) with no traceable primary Google source.
- Authorship/independence of the 28M-response Q4 2025 citation study: reported via ppc.land and Foundation Inc, but Yelp's own marketing uses matching numbers; could not confirm whether Yelp commissioned it. Treat the Yelp-dominance numbers as potentially self-interested.
- No independent (non-vendor) large-scale regression isolating individual GBP fields (photos count, posts frequency, Q&A, service-area settings) against AI-answer inclusion; all field-level weightings found are expert-opinion surveys or heuristics, not measured causal effects. Photos/posts/Q&A specifically: zero quantitative evidence found either way.
- Direct verification of primary texts was impossible: the network egress proxy blocked localfalcon.com, whitespark.ca, support.google.com, blog.google, developers.google.com, prweb.com, searchenginejournal.com, ppc.land — all findings above derive from search-result syntheses of those pages, so exact quotes and some publication dates are unconfirmed.
- Conflicting evidence on rank carryover: onpurposemedia claims AIO-local-pack businesses are "exclusively" traditional map-pack rankers, while Local SEO Data's AI Mode study found 999 AI-only businesses and 48% top-3 overlap — the two claims cover different surfaces (AIO place-card unit vs AI Mode) and could not be reconciled with available data.
- Stats seen only in aggregator summaries without traceable primary attribution: "AI Overviews appear for ~15% of local-intent queries," "~7% of pure local queries / 68% of broader local-language queries with GBP as dominant citation source," and "AI Mode shares 13.7% citation overlap with AI Overviews."

# Lane 3 — Bing / Copilot / ChatGPT search backend (landed 2026-08-08)

FINDINGS

- ChatGPT search launched (Oct 31, 2024) with OpenAI stating the search model works with "third-party search providers" and partners; Microsoft Bing is the primary named provider in OpenAI's docs, alongside OpenAI's own crawlers (OAI-SearchBot for search surfacing, ChatGPT-User for on-demand fetches; neither trains models) → https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-search/ + https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search → Oct 2024, docs still current 2025-2026 → [VENDOR]
- By 2025 ChatGPT is demonstrably not Bing-only: The Information reported (Aug 22, 2025) that OpenAI pulls Google SERP data via scraping service SerpApi for real-time answers, despite Google denying OpenAI index access in 2024 → https://www.webpronews.com/chatgpt-relies-on-google-search-for-real-time-data-via-serpapi/ (relaying The Information) → Aug 2025 → [INDEPENDENT]
- Controlled test by SEO consultant Aleyda Solís (July 2025): a new page indexed in Google but not Bing was answered by ChatGPT word-for-word from Google's SERP snippet; before Google indexed it, ChatGPT search could not find it — evidence ChatGPT uses Google snippets as fallback when Bing lacks coverage → https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/chatgpt-uses-google-serp-snippets-for-answers/ + https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-appears-to-use-google-search-as-a-fallback/552089/ → Jul 2025 → [INDEPENDENT]
- Seer Interactive (Feb 6, 2025; 500+ citations analyzed): 87% of SearchGPT/ChatGPT-search citations matched Bing's top-10 organic results for the same query vs only 56% matching Google's (median Google rank 17) — Bing rank remains the strongest single predictor of ChatGPT citation → https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results → Feb 2025 → [INDEPENDENT]
- Follow-up Seer case study covered by Search Engine Land: ChatGPT decomposes prompts into ~25 fan-out sub-queries searched "in the background using Bing"; brands ranking in Bing's top results for fan-outs dominated brand mentions (68 runs of one hotel prompt) → https://searchengineland.com/bing-ranking-chatgpt-visibility-study-473680 → 2025 → [INDEPENDENT]
- Countervailing data — citation-vs-rank overlap is messy: Ahrefs found only ~12% of AI-cited URLs rank in Google's top-10 for the original prompt, and Surfer SEO's 46M-citation analysis found ~8% ChatGPT-Google top-result overlap; classic rankings (either engine) explain only part of AI citations → https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/ → 2025-2026 → [INDEPENDENT]
- Microsoft Copilot is explicitly Bing-grounded: Copilot Search in Bing launched April 2025, grounding answers on the Bing index via RAG; Microsoft's Nov 7, 2025 Copilot blog added prominent clickable citations and "Show all" sources → https://searchengineland.com/microsoft-officially-launches-copilot-search-in-bing-453958 (Apr 2025) [INDEPENDENT] + https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/blog/2025/11/07/bringing-the-best-of-ai-search-to-copilot/ (Nov 2025) [VENDOR]
- Bing Copilot answers were observed serving map + local business results directly in chat answers (Bing local data), early 2025 → https://www.seroundtable.com/bing-copilot-answers-map-local-results-38964.html → ~Feb-Mar 2025 → [INDEPENDENT]
- Copilot local citations in practice (200 dining-intent prompts across 6 engines): Copilot answered 100% of "best restaurant" queries with citations to restaurant websites + Tripadvisor + OpenTable; across engines, editorial best-of publishers drove ~40% of answers, UGC ~32%, review/reservation platforms ~25% → https://cloro.dev/blog/restaurant-seo/ → 2025-2026 → [VENDOR] (GEO-tool vendor study)
- ChatGPT's local/maps answers are NOT primarily Bing Places: analyses attribute core listing data to Foursquare (one analysis: 70%+ of local results), plus Yelp, with Mapbox rendering the map and Bing's web index as a catch-all; no direct Google Business Profile integration → https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/chatgpt-local-search-data-sources-where-does-business-info-come-from → 2025 → [VENDOR] (methodology not public)
- Bing Places was overhauled Oct 3, 2025: portal migrated from bingplaces.com to bing.com/forbusiness with auto-migration, a rebuilt faster "Import from Google [Business Profile]" tool (GBP-verified businesses often verify instantly; otherwise phone/email/postcard, postcards 7-10 business days), and a new listing-completeness Recommendation Tool; deeper Bing Maps and Copilot integrations planned → https://searchengineland.com/new-bing-places-for-business-is-live-462994 + https://ppc.land/bing-places-for-business-migrates-to-bing-com-domain-with-import-tools/ → Oct 2025 → [INDEPENDENT coverage of vendor launch]
- Claims that "Bing Places powers Copilot local answers / unclaimed listing = invisible in Copilot" are ubiquitous in 2026 SEO-agency guides but rest on inference; one listings vendor concedes "Microsoft has not published how Copilot sources business-specific answers" → https://thestacc.com/blog/bing-places-guide/ + https://www.listingsapi.com/network/microsoft-copilot → 2025-2026 → [VENDOR]
- IndexNow scale (Microsoft's own numbers, Dec 12, 2024): 3.5B URLs/day submitted; 18% of all newly clicked URLs in Bing arrived via IndexNow; May 2025 blog frames IndexNow as a freshness signal grounding Copilot answers → https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/December-2024/ + https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/May-2025/ → Dec 2024 / May 2025 → [VENDOR]
- IndexNow caveat: official FAQ states submission does not guarantee crawling/indexing — it accelerates discovery, not admission. Free implementation paths: Bing Webmaster Tools (up to ~10k URL submissions/day), Yoast/Rank Math/Microsoft WordPress plugins, Cloudflare, Shopify → https://www.indexnow.org/faq → ongoing → [VENDOR FAQ + INDEPENDENT]
- Measurement hook for an "appearance rate" product: Bing Webmaster Tools shipped an AI Performance report (released ~Feb 11, 2026) showing per-site Copilot "grounding queries" and per-page citation counts across Copilot and Bing AI experiences — first major first-party AI-citation dashboard; free for any verified site → https://searchengineland.com/bing-webmaster-tools-ai-performance-report-468751 → Feb 2026 → [INDEPENDENT coverage]
- Consensus 2026 checklist (all steps free): (1) verify site in Bing Webmaster Tools via 2-minute Google Search Console import; (2) submit XML sitemap; (3) enable IndexNow; (4) claim/complete Bing Places via GBP import at bing.com/forbusiness; (5) ensure robots.txt/CDN/WAF do not block Bingbot or OAI-SearchBot; (6) LocalBusiness schema + consistent NAP across Yelp/Tripadvisor/Foursquare; (7) monitor via the AI Performance report → https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/bing-webmaster-tools-chatgpt → 2025-2026 → [VENDOR]

UNKNOWNS

- Could not access primary sources directly (openai.com, help.openai.com, blogs.bing.com, seerinteractive.com, searchengineland.com, learn.microsoft.com egress-blocked); figures relayed via search summaries and secondary coverage.
- No official OpenAI statement quantifying Bing's current share of ChatGPT search retrieval, confirming SerpApi/Google usage, or announcing a proprietary index; "OpenAI is building its own web index" claims are unconfirmed speculation.
- No Microsoft documentation enumerating Copilot's local-answer data sources; the Bing Places → Copilot pipeline is inferred by vendors, not documented; no controlled study quantifies visibility lift from claiming a Bing Places listing.
- The "70%+ of ChatGPT local results come from Foursquare" figure is a single SEO-tool analysis with unpublished methodology.

- Claims of a late-2025/2026 IndexNow "22% of clicked URLs" milestone appear only in low-credibility secondary sources.

# Lane 4 — Reviews & NAP consistency (landed 2026-08-08)

FINDINGS

- ChatGPT recommends only ~1.2% of local business locations (vs. ~35.9% avg appearance in Google 3-Pack); recommended locations averaged 4.3-star ratings, and the four shared traits of AI-recommended locations were accurate/complete data across Google Maps, Yelp, Facebook and brand sites, ratings consistently above 4.0, differentiated local content, and cross-platform consistency rather than strength on one channel → https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/in-ai-driven-discovery-few-brands-are-chosen-most-disappear-302672281.html and https://www.localogy.com/2026/01/soci-lvi-report-reveals-how-ai-is-rewriting-local-search-visibility/ → SOCi Local Visibility Index, released Jan 2026 (350k+ locations, 2,751 brands) → [VENDOR]
